## Suggest a provider

We would love to hear where you want to use CMX. Whether it is another coding agent, an editor extension, or a model service, contact us and we will assess whether an integration is possible.

### Tell us about your setup

- The tool or provider name and a link to its website or integration documentation.
- Your operating system and how you connect to the model today.
- The workflow you want CMX to support and what would make the integration useful.

A short description is enough to start. Please leave out API keys and private conversation or source-code content.

[Suggest a provider by email ↗](mailto:hello@compressi.us?subject=CMX%20provider%20suggestion)

You can also reach us through our [contact page](https://compressi.us/contact).

## What happens next?

We review the available connection options and whether CMX can safely preserve the tool’s behavior. Some integrations may require changes from the tool or provider. A suggestion does not guarantee support or a delivery date, but it helps us understand demand.

Already using OpenCode or Codex? Their setup guides are available in the Providers menu.
