# Source: https://compressi.us/contact

The fastest way to reach the project is email: [hello@compressi.us](mailto:hello@compressi.us). We read everything, though we are a small team and cannot promise an immediate reply.

## Reporting a bug

Help us reproduce the issue quickly by including:

- The CMX version shown by `cmx version`.
- Your operating system and architecture.
- The coding agent or harness you were using.
- The exact steps that led to the problem, and what you expected instead.
- Relevant local logs if you have enabled them. Never send API keys, prompts, source code, or model responses.

## Security disclosures

If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please email us before disclosing it publicly. Include enough detail to reproduce the issue and we will acknowledge receipt as soon as we can. See the [Security](https://compressi.us/security) page for the threat model.

## Privacy requests

To ask what aggregate data is associated with your account, or to request deletion, email us from the address linked to your account. Our [Privacy Policy](https://compressi.us/privacy) explains what little we store.

## What we cannot help with

CMX does not control your model provider. Billing disputes, rate limits, account suspensions, and API outages must be handled with the provider directly. CMX is provided under the [EULA](https://compressi.us/eula) without a service guarantee.
